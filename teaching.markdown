I enjoy teaching and take it seriously. I view teaching as an important responsibility of academics, maybe more important than our research output. I also view teaching as an essential way that I interact with ideas in math.

### Penn Directed Reading Program

The Directed Reading Program at Penn is a fantastic program that pairs undergraduates with graduate students in a semester-long independent study. 

I have had the opportunity to mentor various undergraduates through this program. This has resulted in longterm mentorships with students that exist outside the program. I have mentored the following students throughout my participation in the program.

### Recognition for Teaching

I do not exactly have a teaching philosophy, but there are some guidelines I attempt to abide by when teaching. You can read about them in this blog post.


The Penn math department gives out the <a href="https://www.math.upenn.edu/about/department-history/department-chairs/prizes-and-awards/good-teaching"> Good Teaching Award </a> each semester to TAs. I have received the Good Teaching Award the following semesters.

- Spring 2025: Math 2030 - Introduction to Proofs
- Fall 2024: Math 1200 - Introduction to Calculus

The Penn School of Arts and Sciences gives award to recognize outstanding teachers. Receipts are nominated by their students and department. I was a receipient of the <a href="https://www.college.upenn.edu/news/2026-teaching-awards"> 2026 SAS Dean's Award for Distinguished Teaching by Graduate Students </a>.
